STM32F401RE Evaluation Board - Professional Clean Schematic

Open STM32F401RE_Eval.kicad_pro in KiCad. The schematic is legacy Eeschema format so KiCad may convert it to .kicad_sch on Save. The design intentionally includes explicit NC markers for unused MCU/USB pins, CC1/CC2 Rd resistors, proper reset/user circuits, and power flags for VBUS/GND/VCAP to reduce ERC errors.

Before fabrication: run ERC and DRC in your installed KiCad version and verify footprints/pin mapping against the STM32F401RE datasheet.
